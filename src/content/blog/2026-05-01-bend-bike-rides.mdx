I’ve been looking for a way to give back to the cycling community of my town that I love so much for many years, and it’s finally coming together in the form of [Bend Bike Rides](https://bendbikerides.com): a site for the community to see and report trail conditions, gather on group rides, and more.

This project has been a fun journey of diving into _Geographic Information Systems_ (GIS), postgis, and optimizing mapping libraries to make something incredibly complex not just simple, but _fast_. I’m really proud of how the site has come together and am looking forward to continuing to add more for the community to really come together.
